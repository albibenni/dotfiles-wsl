GlazeWM - Hyprland keybindings
Windows key = left Win / Super. Win+L locks; Alt+L or Win+Right focuses right.

lwin+shift+h, lwin+shift+left : move --direction left
alt+l, lwin+right : focus --direction right
lwin+shift+l, lwin+shift+right : move --direction right

Native Windows shortcuts: Win+V clipboard, Win+Shift+S screenshot, Alt+Tab app switcher.
Pause: Win+Ctrl+Shift+P. Exit: Win+Ctrl+Shift+Escape.
Moving to workspace (Win+Shift+number) does not follow the moved window.
